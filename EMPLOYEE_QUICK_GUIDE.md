# 📋 EMPLOYEE QUICK GUIDE - PRINT THIS!
## How to Use Fingerprint Attendance Machines

---

## 🏢 TWO MACHINES - REMEMBER THIS!

### **Machine 1 - ENTRANCE DOOR** (When Coming)
- Use when: **Arriving at work**
- Records: **IN time**
- Location: **Entrance**

### **Machine 2 - EXIT DOOR** (When Leaving)  
- Use when: **Going home**
- Records: **OUT time**
- Location: **Exit**

---

## ⏰ DAILY ROUTINE

### **Morning**
1. Come to office
2. Go to **Machine 1**
3. Put finger on scanner
4. **Wait for BEEP** ✓
5. You are IN!

### **Evening**
1. Finish work
2. Go to **Machine 2**
3. Put finger on scanner
4. **Wait for BEEP** ✓
5. You are OUT!

---

## 💰 HOW YOU GET PAID

| Your Work | Hours | Status | You Get |
|-----------|-------|--------|---------|
| Full Day | 9+ hours | ✓ PRESENT | **FULL PAY** |
| Half Day | 4.5-9 hours | ⚠ HALF DAY | **HALF PAY** |
| Short Time | Less than 4.5 | ⚠ INCOMPLETE | **MAY LOSE PAY** |
| Forgot Punch | --- | ✗ ABSENT | **NO PAY (LOP)** |

---

## ❌ DON'T MAKE THESE MISTAKES!

### **Mistake 1:** Forgot to punch OUT
→ **Result:** INCOMPLETE - Lose pay!
→ **Fix:** Always punch OUT before leaving

### **Mistake 2:** Used wrong machine
→ **Result:** Wrong attendance!
→ **Fix:** Coming = Machine 1, Leaving = Machine 2

### **Mistake 3:** Worked less than 4.5 hours
→ **Result:** Incomplete day
→ **Fix:** Work minimum 4.5 hours

### **Mistake 4:** Not registered on both machines
→ **Result:** Cannot punch on one machine
→ **Fix:** Tell admin to register you on BOTH

---

## ✅ SIMPLE RULES

### ✓ **DO THIS**
- Punch IN when coming (Machine 1)
- Punch OUT when leaving (Machine 2)
- Work minimum 4.5 hours
- Wait for beep sound
- Clean your finger before scanning

### ✗ **DON'T DO THIS**
- Forget to punch OUT
- Use wrong machine
- Punch many times
- Leave early without punching

---

## 📞 PROBLEMS?

**Machine not working?** → Tell admin immediately  
**Finger not recognized?** → Clean finger and try again  
**Forgot to punch?** → Tell supervisor right away  
**Not sure about hours?** → Ask admin to check
