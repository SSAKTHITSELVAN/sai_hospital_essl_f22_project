# Employee Attendance Guide
## Simple Instructions for Using the Fingerprint Machine

---

## 📍 **Two Machines - Two Different Jobs**

### **Machine 1 - ENTRANCE (When You Come)**
- **Location:** At the entrance door
- **IP Address:** 192.168.1.201
- **Use this when:** You arrive at work
- **This records:** Your IN time

### **Machine 2 - EXIT (When You Leave)**
- **Location:** At the exit door
- **IP Address:** 192.168.1.35
- **Use this when:** You go home
- **This records:** Your OUT time

---

## ⏰ **When to Punch**

### **Morning - When You Arrive**
1. Come to work
2. Go to **Machine 1 (Entrance)**
3. Place your finger on the scanner
4. Wait for beep sound
5. You are now marked as "IN"

### **Evening - When You Leave**
1. Finish your work
2. Go to **Machine 2 (Exit)**
3. Place your finger on the scanner
4. Wait for beep sound
5. You are now marked as "OUT"

---

## 🍽️ **If You Go for Lunch Break (Optional)**

### **Going Out for Lunch**
- Use **Machine 2 (Exit)** - records OUT-1

### **Coming Back from Lunch**
- Use **Machine 1 (Entrance)** - records IN-2

### **Leaving After Work**
- Use **Machine 2 (Exit)** again - records OUT-2

**Note:** If you don't go out for lunch, just punch IN once (morning) and OUT once (evening).

---

## ✅ **How to Get Full Pay - Complete Attendance**

### **Regular Day (9 Hours or More)**
```
Morning:  IN-1 at Machine 1    →  9:00 AM
Evening:  OUT-1 at Machine 2   →  6:00 PM
Result:   9 hours worked       →  FULL PAY ✓
```

### **Half Day (4.5 to 9 Hours)**
```
Morning:  IN-1 at Machine 1    →  9:00 AM
Afternoon: OUT-1 at Machine 2  →  2:00 PM
Result:   5 hours worked       →  HALF DAY PAY
```

### **With Lunch Break (Two Sessions)**
```
Morning:     IN-1 at Machine 1    →  9:00 AM
Lunch Out:   OUT-1 at Machine 2   →  1:00 PM  (4 hours)
Lunch Back:  IN-2 at Machine 1    →  2:00 PM
Evening:     OUT-2 at Machine 2   →  6:00 PM  (4 hours)
Result:      8 hours total        →  HALF DAY PAY
```

## ❌ **Common Mistakes That Reduce Your Pay**

### **Mistake 1: Forgot to Punch OUT**
```
✗ WRONG:
Morning:  IN at 9:00 AM ✓
Evening:  Forgot to punch OUT ✗
Result:   INCOMPLETE - May lose pay!
```
**Fix:** Always punch OUT before going home

---

### **Mistake 2: Used Wrong Machine**
```
✗ WRONG:
Morning:  Used Machine 2 (Exit) by mistake
Evening:  Used Machine 1 (Entrance) by mistake
Result:   System confused - Attendance wrong!
```
**Fix:** 
- Coming = Machine 1 (Entrance)
- Leaving = Machine 2 (Exit)

---

### **Mistake 3: Punched Multiple Times (Duplicate)**
```
✗ WRONG:
9:00 AM - Punched IN
9:05 AM - Punched IN again (worried it didn't work)
Result:  System uses last punch only
```
**Fix:** Punch only ONCE. Wait for beep sound to confirm.

---

### **Mistake 4: Working Less Than 4.5 Hours**
```
✗ WRONG:
IN at 9:00 AM → OUT at 12:00 PM (only 3 hours)
Result: INCOMPLETE - Less than half day
```
**Fix:** Work minimum 4.5 hours for half day pay

---

### **Mistake 5: Not Registered on Both Machines**
```
✗ WRONG:
Your finger registered only on Machine 1
Cannot punch on Machine 2 when leaving
Result: Incomplete attendance
```
**Fix:** Tell admin to register your finger on BOTH machines

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

### **Problem: Machine not recognizing my finger**
**Solution:** 
- Clean your finger
- Press firmly but gently
- Try different angle
- If still not working, tell admin immediately

### **Problem: I forgot to punch OUT yesterday**
**Solution:**
- Tell your supervisor/admin immediately
- They can check the records
- May need manual correction

### **Problem: Machine shows error or beeps differently**
**Solution:**
- Try again once
- If still error, tell admin
- Don't punch multiple times

### **Problem: Not sure if punch was recorded**
**Solution:**
- If you heard the beep = Successfully recorded
- If no beep = Not recorded, try again
- Ask admin to check attendance page
